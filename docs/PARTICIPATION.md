# Participation in the ordinary points candidate

> The current release candidate is V2.0, internally identified as `points-204040-v5` (policy/5). Historical policy identifiers remain unchanged for verification.

For `points-204040-v5`, register a hotkey, maintain control of its owning coldkey,
and make correctly attributed contributions against actual earnings. No upfront
NOAH purchase or entry deposit is required. The initial score is 0.2 points;
verified daily calculations, publication and chain consensus determine rewards.

The target is 50% of eligible earnings received on the previous UTC day.
Native NOAH is not mandatory: eligible foreign subnet tokens, root TAO or a mixture
can satisfy it after receipt verification and valuation. Registration collateral
and its return are separate from earnings. Contributed assets belong to the Ark;
read the [deposit notices](DEPOSIT_NOTICES.md) before sending.

Read [ordinary scoring](ORDINARY_SCORING.md) for the complete candidate rules,
[the miner quickstart](MINER_QUICKSTART.md) to participate, and
[contribution attribution](CONTRIBUTIONS.md) to avoid an uncredited payment.
This candidate is not an announcement of activation. Older policy descriptions
are retained in [historical participation](LEGACY_PARTICIPATION.md).
