# Before you deposit

An Ark contribution gives up ownership of stake. Read these five notices before
approving a transfer. The deposit helper displays the same notices before it
asks you to confirm.

## 1. Permanent contribution

Treat this deposit as permanent. It transfers ownership of your stake to
the Ark. Noah provides no withdrawal or refund process.

## 2. Chain rules

Deposits remain subject to Bittensor's chain rules. Future changes to
those rules or governance decisions could affect the Ark and its holdings.

## 3. Your wallet signs

The helper builds a transaction for your approval. Your configured wallet
signs it on your computer, and the chain processes it. Do not give Noah
your recovery phrase or private keys.

## 4. Check the transfer

Transfers must be enabled for the token you deposit. Check the network,
Ark receiving address, validator delegation and amount before signing.

## 5. Mining rewards

The Ark does not repay your deposit or promise you a financial return.
Qualifying contributions can affect your Noah mining score. Rewards and
continued ownership of a miner seat are not guaranteed.

## What makes a contribution count

Current Mainnet starter eligibility requires no upfront NOAH purchase or Ark
deposit. The ongoing target remains **50% of eligible spendable mining earnings**,
including starter earnings. Any preserved legacy entry credit is separate and
does not pay down that target. Returned registration collateral is not mining
earnings. See [starter entry](STARTER_ENTRY.md) for eligibility and limits.

A contribution must reach the [Ark receiving address](CONTRIBUTIONS.md#address-map),
with the required Ark Validator delegation, and identify the miner it should
credit. Staking to the Ark Validator while keeping ownership does not count.
A transfer to the wrong destination or validator can move real funds without
earning mining credit.

A finalized transaction proves that the transfer completed. It does not, by
itself, prove that the validator credited it or changed your score. Check the
published contribution records and the scoring day that processed it.

Read the [miner guide](MINER_QUICKSTART.md) for the steps and the
[contribution rules](CONTRIBUTIONS.md) for attribution and catch-up payments.
