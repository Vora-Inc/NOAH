# Scoring rules by version

> Mainnet uses V2.1 (`points-204040-v6`, policy/6) from November 24, 2026 at 00:00 UTC (scoring day 1345). V2 (`points-204040-v5`, policy/5) remains the policy for its earlier results.

Mainnet V2 and V2.1 use [ordinary 20 / 40 / 40 scoring](ORDINARY_SCORING.md).
It has no starter pool and no new-miner entry payment. The linked specification
includes the exact formula, token valuation, maturity, daily consistency, debt,
prepayment, catch-up and publication behavior, with examples.

This is source documentation, not proof of live activation. Read the mode and
policy in a retained calculation and verify its publication on chain.
[Historical scoring](LEGACY_SCORING.md) remains available to reproduce older records.
Do not apply its decay or starter rules to ordinary V2 or V2.1 scoring.
